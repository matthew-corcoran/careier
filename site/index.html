The problem

Carers lift people all shift. Nobody measures how.

Manual handling is the leading cause of injury in health and social care, and back injuries drive the absences and turnover care homes can't afford.

How it works

From the bedside to the manager's screen
01 · Sense

Vest + camera

Trunk tilt from the vest. REBA and the patient's neck from the camera coach.

02 · Segment

Every lift

Each transfer is cut out of the shift with its full motion window.

03 · Reason

Care-ER

Labels goal, phases and the risky moment, the way Gemini 4 Argon labels robot data.

04 · Act

Recommend

Today, actions for managers. Next, exoskeletons. Later, care robots.
